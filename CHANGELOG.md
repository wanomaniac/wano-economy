# First release of Wano's economy mod.
## New features such as:
* Rewritten to no longer depend on the architectuary mod, meaning that all you need is just the mod loader and the mod itself.
* in game trading through inventories seamlessly with the exchange of cash as a additional feature.
* Auctioning your inventory to the entire server in exchange for money. With all auctions being publicly available.

## Upcoming features in the future
* Proper filtering for history
* Multiple auctioneers in one auction support
